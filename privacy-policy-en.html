2. Information and purposes

  • Account: identifiers, email, profile information and authentication state obtained through Google sign-in, for identity verification, account-specific data management and purchase/trial eligibility.
  • Location and routes: permitted current location, searches, destination addresses and coordinates, departure/arrival times, dwell times, transport and calculation settings, and route results, for maps, geocoding, search, route creation and optimization. Location is requested for the feature in use; the app is not intended for continuous background tracking.
  • Contacts and photos: entered or selected names, phone numbers, addresses, companies, coordinates and photos, for contact management. With permission, the import feature reads device contacts as candidates and brings selected contacts into the registration flow.
  • AI inputs and contact candidates: your text, current/departure times, travel mode, dwell time, road-avoidance and other calculation settings, home name/address and available current coordinates are used for route creation and estimates. To match place names, the request includes app contact candidates up to an implementation limit, with registration IDs, names, companies, addresses, phone numbers, home flags and location-availability flags. Candidates may include contacts not mentioned in your request. The device phonebook is not directly sent in full to AI, but contacts imported into the app can be included as candidates. Photo image data is not included in this text request. Information you type into the prompt is also transmitted.
  • Purchases and use: order IDs, purchase tokens, products, currencies, amounts, entitlements, trial history, balances, estimates, reservations, usage records, refund requests and their history, for verification, accounting, balance/charge settlement, refunds and fraud prevention. Google Play handles payment details such as card numbers.
  • Device and operations: installation/device identifiers, sessions, request times, processing/error and audit logs, for security, diagnostics, fraud prevention and preventing unauthorized account sharing.
  • Settings and backups: appearance, language, home and other preferences, contacts, routes and associated backup information, for saving and restoring data.

3. Storage and external services

Contacts, routes and settings are stored locally. Relevant information is processed externally when you use authentication, billing, AI, maps or backups. Connections can also disclose IP addresses and technical request information to recipients. AI requests include the contact candidates described above, not just the text you enter.

  • Google, Firebase and Google Cloud: sign-in, Firebase Authentication, and Cloud Run/Firestore processing of access, purchases, balances, usage and refunds.
  • Google Maps Platform: map, Places, Geocoding and Routes services receive searches, addresses, coordinates and travel conditions. Exporting HTML with a map sends coordinates, viewport and stored route shapes to Google Maps Static API to generate the map image. Starting guidance in Google Maps passes destinations/waypoints to that app.
  • AI service (Google Gemini): request content is sent through the operator's server to configured Google Gemini, including token-count processing for estimates. Responses support routes, display and settlement. Before sending personal data, the operator is responsible for verifying necessity, contracts/settings excluding independent training use, and appropriate processor oversight. Warnings do not transfer that responsibility to users. Any independent provider use requires its own lawful basis, disclosures/consents; information without that basis is not to be transmitted. Provider security/legal recordkeeping means zero retention is not guaranteed.
  • Google Play: information needed for purchases, order verification, entitlements and refunds.
  • Google Drive and sharing: backups are saved to the destination you choose. HTML/CSV exports and shared files contain selected information and are controlled by the recipient after sharing. Check the audience before publishing files containing locations or addresses.

Providers may process data abroad; outsourcing alone does not remove Japan's foreign-transfer requirements. The operator must check contracting entities, processing countries, access arrangements and safeguards, and establish an applicable lawful basis, such as informed consent or a system maintaining equivalent measures. Consent-based transfers require prior explanation of countries, their protection systems and recipient measures. Equivalent measures require continuing verification and information on request as required by law. Ask the contact channel about recipients, countries and legal grounds.

Domestic processing is outsourced only within necessary purposes with appropriate supervision. Disclosures outside statutory exceptions such as outsourcing normally require consent or another lawful basis. Disclosures may occur under lawful requests or other exceptions protecting life, body or property. We do not sell personal information or provide it for targeted advertising.

4. Permissions and choices

Location supports current-location display, registration and routing. Contacts access supports phonebook import. Photo access supports registration and display of selected photos. These use Android permission/selection controls. You can change permissions in device settings; relevant features may become unavailable. Contacts permission does not grant access to call history or SMS.

Backups, AI requests, exports and sharing follow your actions. Do not include information you do not want transmitted in prompts or contact candidates. Ensure you have authority to handle other people's information. Do not submit passwords, payment details, health information or other unnecessary sensitive information to AI. Revoking permissions or logging out does not delete information already stored or transmitted.

5. Retention and security

  • Local information: contacts, routes, history and settings remain until deleted, app data is cleared or equivalent action is taken. Switching accounts or signing out does not automatically delete other accounts' stored information.
  • Backups and exports: Android backup/device transfer can restore data. Copies in Drive, exported files and shared destinations are managed/deleted by you or the destination administrator.
  • Server records: account, entitlement, trial, balance, estimate, usage, refund and audit records are retained as needed for service delivery, settlement, fraud prevention, refunds/disputes and applicable legal recordkeeping. Necessity is assessed by record category, outstanding transactions and legal obligations. Records no longer needed are deleted or de-identified. No single automatic-deletion period is promised.
  • External providers: authentication, map, AI, payment and cloud providers also apply their retention conditions.

We use HTTPS/encrypted transport, authentication and access controls. This does not guarantee that every local record/export is independently encrypted or that security is absolute. We investigate identified issues, take appropriate action and provide legally required notifications.
